Prerequisites & Concept Mapping ID: PH-11-KIN-04

Ensure thorough grasp of quadratic resolving vectors along perpendicular axes prior to tackling inclined planes. If gravitational components on non-orthogonal planes feel unclear, revisit Vector Decomposition before advancing to Section 2.5.

lightbulb Core Postulate

The horizontal and vertical components of projectile motion are completely independent under uniform gravitational acceleration g.

Key Standard Equations Uniform Gravity
Time of Flight T = (2u · sin θ) / g
Maximum Height H = (u² · sin²θ) / 2g
Horizontal Range R = (u² · sin 2θ) / g
warning Common JEE Pitfall

On an inclined plane with inclination α, effective acceleration changes:
a_x = g sin α and a_y = -g cos α. Do not take horizontal acceleration as zero!
